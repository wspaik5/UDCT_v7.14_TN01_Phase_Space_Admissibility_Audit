## Interpretation
The p=1.5 cancellation concerns a leading asymptotic inversion coefficient under specified isolated spherical assumptions. The r_a/a≈0.466466787396 candidate is a finite-window Osipkov–Merritt tangency, not a universal anisotropy threshold. The turning-point label r/a≈0.47409755 is a different quantity. Global DF positivity and the equality-case full DF sign remain unproved. Negative DF is a model-specific equilibrium admissibility failure, not a universal MOND falsification.

The 3D Hessian, spherical Jeans, and spherical DF calculations are distinct branches. Hydrus I is not directly fitted with individual-star line-of-sight likelihoods here. Bullet Cluster tests require a new non-equilibrium 3D formulation rather than transferring spherical thresholds.
